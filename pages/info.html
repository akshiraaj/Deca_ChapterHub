Getting Started

What is a role-play?

A timed scenario where you're given a business problem and a role (e.g. marketing manager). You get prep time, then present a solution to a judge and answer follow-up questions.

What is a written case study?

A longer business problem you research and prepare a written report and presentation for, usually with a partner. Judged on analysis, strategy, and delivery.

The exam

A multiple-choice test on business concepts specific to your competitive event's category (marketing, finance, hospitality, etc.).

Competition Levels

Chapter

Internal practice and points-earning events run by execs throughout the year.

Region / District

The first official competitive level — top performers qualify for States.

State (SCDC)

State Career Development Conference. Top finishers qualify for ICDC.

International (ICDC)

The international finals — the biggest stage in DECA.

Prep Tips

Practice role-plays weekly

Time yourself doing prep + presenting. Comfort with the format matters as much as content knowledge.

Know your performance indicators

Every event has a published list of skills you'll be judged on — study them directly.

Use Google Classroom

Practice exams, past role-play prompts, and slide decks are kept there — check it weekly during competition season.
